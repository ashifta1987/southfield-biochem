The Kerala ingredient

Made with Kerala coconut oil — the oil this land is named after

Kerala takes its name from kera, the Malayalam word for coconut tree. For generations, families here have pressed coconut oil at home and used it for cooking, hair, massage and skin. Our glycerin bathing bars are enriched with the same coconut oil, so every bar carries a little of Kerala's daily life — gentle, nourishing and familiar.

Cold-pressed from Kerala coconuts Blended with pure glycerin No harsh stripping agents
Lauric acid Around half of coconut oil is lauric acid — it gives soap a rich, fast lather and has natural cleansing properties.
Moisture Coconut oil leaves a light, protective layer on the skin; paired with humectant glycerin it helps skin stay soft after bathing.
Vitamin E Naturally present antioxidants help calm dry, sun-exposed skin — welcome in Kerala's heat and the Gulf's dry air alike.
Gentle Mild enough for daily use by the whole family, from children to elders — the way coconut oil has always been used in Kerala homes.

About Southfield Biochem

A Kerala soap house — from a small handmade batch in 2016 to a 30-tonne-a-day certified plant

Southfield Biochem Private Limited makes glycerin and handmade bathing soap, body wash and home-care products at Pattambi in Palakkad, the heart of Kerala's coconut and turmeric country. We began in 2016 as Majlis Industries, a small glycerin soap-making unit in Kerala; as the business grew we built our own factory and re-formed it as Southfield Biochem Private Limited in December 2019. Today the plant turns out 10 tonnes of transparent glycerin soap, 10 tonnes of liquids and 5 tonnes of washing powder a day — alongside small, hand-poured cold-process batches that keep us close to the craft we started with.

Our own brands — YGA glycerin bars, Cryztal luxury bars, Faabi home care and Exman cleaners — are sold through Kerala's shops, supermarkets, a network of local sales partners, our BathandCare Factory Outlet in Kochi and our online store bathandcare.com. The same lines manufacture private label, OEM and ODM products for retail chains, brand owners and online sellers, and export orders for importers in the UAE, Qatar, Oman and Kuwait.

Everything we make is tested in our in-house laboratory before release, on a line certified to ISO 9001:2015 and GMP ISO 22716, under a Kerala Drugs Control cosmetics licence. We are a registered MSME, an FIEO member exporter and hold a Free Sale Certificate for our bathing bars.
